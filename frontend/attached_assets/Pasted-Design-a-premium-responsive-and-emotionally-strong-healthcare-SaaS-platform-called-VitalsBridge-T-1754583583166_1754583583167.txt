Design a premium, responsive, and emotionally strong healthcare SaaS platform called VitalsBridge. This product enables remote family health monitoring, smart vitals tracking, emergency alerts, doctor collaboration, and ePharmacy ordering — all in one beautifully animated and accessible dashboard.

🎨 Brand & Design Language

Color palette:

Background: #ffffff (primary), #F0F9FF (cards)

Primary accent: #007BFF (blue)

Alerts: #EF4444 (Red), #FACC15 (Yellow), #22C55E (Green)

Fonts: Poppins for headings, Inter or Roboto for body; Roboto Mono for vitals

Style: Soft-glass UI with shadows, rounded corners (radius-xl), grid-based layout

Animations: Use Lottie, Framer Motion, or GSAP; for 3D: Spline or Three.js

Core Features (scroll-reveal cards with icons & subtle motion)

→ Classify abnormal readings
